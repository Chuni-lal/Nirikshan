Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Rule 6: Declarations to be made on every package

Every package shall bear thereon or on label securely affixed thereto, a definite, plain and conspicuous declaration as to:

(a) Name and address of the manufacturer: Name and complete address of manufacturer/packer/importer.

(b) Net quantity: Weight, measure or number of commodity contained in the package in standard metric units.

(d) Month and year: Month and year in which commodity is manufactured, packed or imported.

(e) Retail sale price: Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes.

(f) Country of origin: Mandatory declaration of country of origin for all packaged commodities.

(g) Consumer Care details: Name, address, telephone number, and email address of consumer complaint officer.
