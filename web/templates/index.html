Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Mandatory Statutory Declarations, Rule Clauses & Consumer Awareness Guide (DoCA)

Rule 6(1)(a) PCR 2011

Name & Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer

Statutory Clause: Complete postal address with pincode of the manufacturer, packer, or importer.

Consumer Meaning: Fixes legal accountability and enables consumers to trace product origin.

Nirīkshan Check: Validates address line, PIN code, and registered entity name via OCR.
Rule 6(1)(b) PCR 2011

Generic or Common Name of Commodity

Statutory Clause: Generic or common name of the commodity contained inside.

Consumer Meaning: Prevents deceptive brand names masking true composition.

Nirīkshan Check: Matches title with official FSSAI & Legal Metrology lists.
Rule 6(1)(c) PCR 2011

Net Quantity Declaration & Standard SI Units

Statutory Clause: Net weight/volume/count in SI metric units (`g`, `kg`, `ml`, `L`, `N`).

Consumer Meaning: Guarantees consumers get exact measure paid for without ambiguous shorthand (`gms`, `kilos`).

Nirīkshan Check: Validates numeric weight, unit symbol, and font height ratio.
Rule 6(1)(d) PCR 2011

Date of Mfg / Packing / Import & Expiry

Statutory Clause: Month and year of packing/import + mandatory Expiry date for food items.

Consumer Meaning: Protects consumers from consuming expired or stale products.

Nirīkshan Check: Extracts `MM/YYYY` format and checks remaining shelf life.
Rule 6(1)(e) PCR 2011

Maximum Retail Price (MRP) & Unit Sale Price (USP)

Statutory Clause: "MRP ₹ xx.xx (inclusive of all taxes)" + mandatory Unit Sale Price.

Consumer Meaning: Prevents overcharging over printed MRP and enables cost comparison across pack sizes.

Nirīkshan Check: Verifies tax inclusion phrase, MRP limits, and USP calculation.
Rule 6(1)(f) PCR 2011

Country of Origin Declaration

Statutory Clause: Compulsory explicit statement of "Country of Origin" for imported packages.

Consumer Meaning: Full transparency on product origin for informed decision-making.

Nirīkshan Check: Flags missing country of origin tags on imported goods.
Rule 6(1)(n) PCR 2011

Customer Care & Grievance Contact Details

Statutory Clause: Name, address, helpline telephone number, and email of grievance officer.

Consumer Meaning: Direct helpline channel for complaints regarding defective goods.

Nirīkshan Check: Validates email syntax, 10-digit helpline phone, and address.
Rule 7 & 8 PCR 2011

Minimum Font & Letter Height Ratios

Statutory Clause: Minimum letter height based on package surface area (1mm up to 6mm).

Consumer Meaning: Prevents printing mandatory warnings in unreadable microscopic text.

Nirīkshan Check: Bounding box DPI measurement for statutory text letter height.
FSSAI Mandate Food Safety

FSSAI License No. & Veg / Non-Veg Logo

Statutory Clause: 14-digit FSSAI License Number and green dot (Veg) / brown dot (Non-Veg) symbol.

Consumer Meaning: Verifies food safety authorization and dietary preferences.

Nirīkshan Check: Validates 14-digit FSSAI number and detects dietary dot symbol.

Department of Consumer Affairs & Legal Metrology Directives

1. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 Policy

All pre-packaged commodities sold across India must declare MRP (inclusive of all taxes), Net Quantity, Date of Manufacture/Import, Manufacturer Name & Address, Country of Origin, and Customer Care Details on the Principal Display Panel in legible font sizes.

2. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 Directive

Safeguards consumers against unfair trade practices, misleading advertisements, overcharging above declared MRP, and non-compliant packaging declarations across retail and e-commerce platforms.

3. Digital India Data Security & Privacy Policy

All uploaded packaging label images and extracted audit data are processed locally with 100% offline capability, ensuring zero external API transmission and full data security compliance.

About Nirīkshan — Packaged Commodity Compliance Auditor

SIH26034 Prototype • B.Tech 3rd Sem (Big-6 Group), Sushant University

Project Overview & Team Credits

This application is an automated compliance verification prototype developed by the B.Tech 3rd Semester students (Big-6 Group) of Sushant University for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26034).

Core Functionality

Nirīkshan checks packaged commodities against the mandatory statutory declarations required under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. Using advanced computer vision and OCR, it verifies mandatory declarations (Manufacturer Details, Net Quantity, MRP, Manufacturing Date, Country of Origin, and Customer Care Info) alongside letter height font analysis — instantly generating a clear PASS or FAIL compliance verdict with court-ready photographic evidence.

Scalability & Future Roadmap

This project is built on a highly modular and scalable architecture (FastAPI, EasyOCR, OpenCV). It is currently in its active testing phase. In future iterations, this platform will scale to provide end-to-end compliance services accessible directly to everyday consumer citizens as well as on-field enforcement inspectors across India.

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations

Every packaged commodity must declare the following 6 mandatory fields on the principal display panel:

(a) Manufacturer Name & Address: Complete postal address with pincode.

(b) Net Quantity: Standard metric unit (g, kg, ml, l, pcs).

(c) Maximum Retail Price (MRP): "MRP inclusive of all taxes".

(d) Date of Mfg/Packing/Import: Month and year of packing.

(e) Customer Care Info: Telephone number and official email address.

(f) Country of Origin: Mandatory declaration of country of origin.
